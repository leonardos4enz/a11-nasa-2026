# Using the Burning Activity Calendar

## Choosing an area
Use the search box at the top to pick an area (filter by Mexico or the United States). "Draw area" lets you click a
polygon on the map; "Coordinates" creates a circle of 1-150 km around a latitude and longitude. Drawn areas are
computed on the fly from the database.

## The timeline
The strip at the bottom has one bar per month from January 2001 to the current month, coloured by hotspot count. Click
a month to select it, use the arrows to jump to the previous or next month with fires, zoom with + and - or the mouse
wheel, and press Play to animate through time. The shaded band marks the MODIS era, whose counts are estimated.

## Is this month unusual?
The Overview tab compares the selected month with the same calendar month in the baseline years (2003-2025, excluding
the month's own year). Categories: Record high (above every past value), Much above normal (90th percentile or more),
Above normal (67th-90th), Normal, Below normal (10th-33rd) and Much below normal (10th or less). When the typical value
is under 3 or most past years had zero fires, the month is "Too few fires to judge". The current month is compared
with the same number of days of past months, so a half month is not called low just because it is not over.

## The other tabs
- Season: a heatmap of every month and year, to see when the fire season starts, peaks and ends.
- Burn: an agricultural burning calendar. Choose the months in which burns would be allowed and the page shows what
  share of a typical year's hotspots falls in them (each month shows its percentage and the peak month is marked),
  together with the country's burn regulation when there is one.
- Method: how the counts were harmonized and how well MODIS and VIIRS agree in this area.
- Report: pick a date range, compare detected hotspots with officially reported fires, and download a CSV of the
  hotspots, the monthly series, or PNG images of the calendar and charts.

## Map layers
Hotspots shows the real detections of the selected month (with a Filter by fire type and FRP), Area outline shows the
area of interest, and 72 h forecast shows the forecast cells.

## Asking Ember
Ember, the assistant in the right panel, answers questions about the data and the project and can also use the page
for you: "busca Ocosingo", "pon abril 2024", "open the season tab", "show the forecast layer", "dark mode". You see
its cursor do each step, and you can press Stop at any time.
