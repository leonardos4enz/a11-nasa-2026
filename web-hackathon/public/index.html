Cross-validation by year

A local check of the link the method calibrates: Aqua (13:30 overpass, like VIIRS) to VIIRS (S-NPP, NOAA-20 from 2018-04; NOAA-20 only where there is no S-NPP). Each year is predicted with the ratio of the other years of 2012–2021 (leave-one-year-out), so the check never sees the year it tests. Years from 2022 are left out because the MODIS orbits drift. It uses one ratio per area; the calendar uses one national factor per calendar month.

How the record is harmonized

Why a ratio. VIIRS (375 m) sees smaller fires than MODIS (1 km), so it reports several times more hotspots for the same fires, but both follow the same season. One factor per calendar month (VIIRS ÷ Aqua, median of 2012–2021) puts 25 years on the VIIRS scale.

Uncertainty. MODIS-era months (before 2012-02) carry a low–high range: the interquartile range of the yearly VIIRS ÷ Aqua ratios for that month. VIIRS months are observed and have no range.

Limitations. The factors are national (Mexico) and apply everywhere, so a small area whose fires differ from the country average can be off. The United States has no S-NPP data here, so its record is Aqua × k through 2018-03.

What's in the files?

Hotspots CSV: one row per satellite detection (vegetation fires) inside the area and range, all satellites: acq_date, acq_time (UTC HHMM), latitude, longitude, sensor, satellite, confidence, conf_class, frp, brightness, bright_2, scan, track, daynight, fire_type, processing, version. Up to 200,000 rows.

Monthly CSV: one row per month in the range: area, year, month, count, sensor_era, harmonized, low, high, baseline_median, percentile, category.

About the data

Hotspots are satellite detections, not confirmed fires. They include agricultural burns and will not match official fire counts.

One consistent record. MODIS-era years are adjusted to the VIIRS scale so years can be compared; those years carry an estimate range. See the Method tab.

"Normal" means the middle third of the same month in the baseline years, for the same days when the month is still in progress.

Source: NASA FIRMS (MODIS C6.1, VIIRS 375 m). We acknowledge the use of data from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS), part of NASA's LANCE and ESDIS.
